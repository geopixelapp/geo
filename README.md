## 🤖 How Does GeoPixel Work?

GeoPixel uses AI to analyze visual clues in a photograph and help identify where it might have been taken.

Upload an image, let the AI examine its visual characteristics, and explore the locations that could match what appears in the picture.

The idea is simple: turn curiosity into an interactive location investigation.

## ✨ Features That Make GeoPixel Interesting

### 📸 1. AI-Powered Photo Location Analysis

Upload a photo and let AI examine details such as architecture, landscapes, road markings, vegetation, and other visual clues that may help identify a location.

### 🌎 2. Explore Possible Locations on a Map

Go beyond a simple text answer. Explore suggested locations on an interactive map and investigate the surrounding area.

### 🔍 3. Confidence and Explanations

Location identification isn't always straightforward. GeoPixel is designed to provide context for its predictions, helping users understand the clues behind a result rather than simply receiving a place name.

### 🧠 4. Multiple Location Possibilities

Sometimes several places look remarkably similar. Exploring alternative hypotheses can help you compare possibilities instead of relying entirely on a single guess.

### 🗺️ 5. Street View Exploration

Take your investigation further by exploring matching areas through Street View when available. Compare buildings, streets, landmarks, and other visible details to see whether a suggested location makes sense.

### 🌐 6. A Simple, Accessible Experience

GeoPixel brings photo analysis and location exploration together in one place, making it easier to investigate an image without jumping between multiple tools.

## 💡 Why I Built GeoPixel

The world is full of fascinating places, and photographs often contain more clues than we initially notice.

I wanted to build something that combines AI, visual analysis, and interactive maps to make that discovery process more accessible.

GeoPixel is an ongoing project focused on making photo-based location discovery an engaging experience.

It's not about pretending that every image has one obvious answer. Similar-looking places, limited visual information, and edited photographs can make location identification difficult. AI predictions should be treated as possibilities to investigate, not guaranteed facts.

The goal is to help people explore those possibilities.
